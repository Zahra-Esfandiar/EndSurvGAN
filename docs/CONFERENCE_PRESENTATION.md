## EndSurvGAN: End-to-End Survival-Aware Generative Modeling of Censored Time-to-Event Data

### Recognition

✅ Accepted as an **Oral Presentation** at the **18th Iranian Statistics Conference (2026)**

---

## Presentation Overview

This presentation introduces EndSurvGAN, a survival-aware generative framework designed for generating synthetic censored time-to-event data while preserving important survival characteristics.

The presentation covers:

- Motivation behind synthetic survival data generation
- Challenges of censored time-to-event datasets
- Survival-aware generative modeling strategy
- Model architecture and optimization framework
- Evaluation methodology and experimental design

---

## Key Research Message

Unlike conventional synthetic data generators, EndSurvGAN explicitly considers:

- Survival time dynamics
- Event/censoring mechanisms
- Predictor-survival relationships
- Downstream survival analysis utility
