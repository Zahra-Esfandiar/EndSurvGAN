# 🎓 Academic Impact

## EndSurvGAN Research Contribution

EndSurvGAN represents a research effort at the intersection of:

- 🧬 Survival Analysis
- 📊 Statistical Machine Learning
- 🤖 Generative Modeling
- 🏥 Healthcare AI

## Why It Matters

Healthcare datasets often contain limited access, privacy restrictions, and incomplete follow-up. Synthetic data generation can support reproducible research while maintaining important statistical properties.

EndSurvGAN focuses on preserving survival-specific structures rather than treating time-to-event data as ordinary tabular data.

## Research Directions

Future extensions include:

- Competing risks generation
- Recurrent event modeling
- Privacy-aware synthetic healthcare data
- Uncertainty-aware survival generation
